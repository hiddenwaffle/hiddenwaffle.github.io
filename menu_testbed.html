Menu lab

Settings rows with arrows (Music, Effects, Text speed, Reduce motion, Print filter) work the same in every variant, as the game does since 26 Oct: a tap on the words selects the row and shows its explanation; a tap on the value between the arrows steps it on, back to the start past the end; the arrows step it down or up and stop at the ends. Keyboard: ← → step it, Enter steps it on.

Keyboard: ↑ ↓ or W S choose, ← → change pages, Enter/Space confirm, Esc back. Tab reaches lab controls. Gamepad: D-pad/left stick, A confirm, B cancel.

Input notes

Local readout only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
